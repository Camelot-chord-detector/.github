## About Camelot

Camelot is a focused music analysis tool for DJs, producers, and listeners who want to understand the harmonic structure of their tracks. Camelot helps you detect chords, identify musical keys, and organize your library around harmonic mixing principles. Whether you are preparing a live set, studying a song, or building a consistent DJ library, Camelot keeps key and chord information readable and ready for real decisions.

Camelot works with the Camelot wheel system, a popular method for harmonic mixing that assigns keys to numbered positions. When you run Camelot music analysis on a track, you see its key, compatible keys, and chord progression in one clear view. That means less guessing when you transition between songs, and more confidence when you plan a set that flows.

For producers and students, Camelot chord detector breaks down the harmonic content of a recording so you can study progressions, compare sections, and understand how a track is built. For working DJs, Camelot key finder turns a messy library into a structured map where every track has a place on the wheel.

Camelot keeps the workflow simple: load a track, run analysis, review the key and chord results, then use that information to tag, sort, or plan your next mix. The interface stays calm and readable, so you can focus on the music instead of fighting with complex menus.

## Why Camelot

Most music players show you a waveform and a title, but they do not tell you whether two tracks will sound good together. Camelot solves that gap by putting harmonic information first. Instead of guessing or relying on memory, you can check the key and chord data before you commit to a transition.

Camelot is built for people who take their music seriously but do not want a complicated studio tool. It is light enough for everyday library work and detailed enough for study and preparation. If you have ever wondered why two songs clash or why a certain pair sounds perfect, Camelot gives you the vocabulary to understand it.

The Camelot wheel is at the center of the experience. Each key gets a number and a letter, and compatible keys sit next to each other. Camelot key finder places your tracks on that wheel, so you can see at a glance which songs are safe to mix and which ones will create tension.

Camelot also supports chord detection for deeper analysis. You can review chord progressions, compare them across tracks, and use that knowledge to build sets with intentional energy. For DJs who want to move beyond simple beatmatching, Camelot harmonic mixing is the next step.
